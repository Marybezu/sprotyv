# OpenMoji

Colour emoji from OpenMoji (https://openmoji.org, https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji),
by the OpenMoji project at HfG Schwäbisch Gmünd, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Kept here unchanged, renamed by meaning:

siren 1F6A8, house 1F3E0, hand 270B, person 1F9CD, fire 1F525, teddy 1F9F8, key 1F511, star 2B50.

`scripts/pictograms/openmoji.py` turns them into Android vector drawables `om_<name>.xml`.
